A blog on managing AI in your company

I write for founders, CEOs and executive teams already using artificial intelligence — but who need to manage it. Each article addresses one concrete decision: whom to put in charge of the AI agenda, what to permit and what to stop, how to meet the regulation and how to evidence that the investment paid off.

This is not a roundup of AI tool news. Tools change every month; the questions leadership faces stay the same. I write only about what I have verified in real operations or with my own capital — and when I am not certain about something, I say so.
