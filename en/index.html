Most companies are not providers of a high-risk AI system. What most often applies to you is Article 4 and, depending on how you deploy AI, also Article 50.

Article 4 (AI literacy) has applied since 2 February 2025. Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 — the Digital Omnibus on AI, which entered into force on 27 July 2026 — changed its wording: you do not have to guarantee a specific level of literacy for every individual, but you must still take appropriate measures to support its development. It is an obligation of effort, not of guaranteed result.

The transparency obligations under Article 50 generally apply from 2 August 2026, depending on the company's role and how AI is used. For providers of systems generating synthetic content that were on the market before that date, a transitional period until 2 December 2026 applies to Article 50(2).

The main obligations for high-risk systems apply from 2 December 2027 (Annex III) and from 2 August 2028 (Annex I).
